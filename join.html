Good questions

FAQ

Who can join the flagship camp?

Any child in Class 4 or 5 (roughly ages 9 to 11), from any school. Mixing schools is part of the fun.

When and where is it?

Three consecutive days over winter break, 9:30 AM to 1:30 PM. The venue and dates go to the interest list first.

How much does it cost?

One all-inclusive price for all three days: staff, materials, snacks, passport, certificates and keepsakes. Pricing is shared with the interest list first.

Is it safe?

Every counselor is background-verified per POCSO Act requirements, there’s 1 counselor per 10 kids, first-aid on site every day, and nut-free snacks as standard.

What exactly will they do?

Build, explore, trade and team up across three themed worlds, with a new hands-on challenge every day.

Will there be photos?

Only with your explicit consent. Never without it.
